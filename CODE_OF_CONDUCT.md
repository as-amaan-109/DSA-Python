# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, inclusive and positive learning environment for everyone participating in the **Python DSA Placement Preparation** project.

This repository is created for learning programming, sharing knowledge, improving problem-solving skills and preparing for technical placements.

We expect all contributors, participants and community members to interact professionally and respectfully.

Our goal is to make this project a safe and supportive environment where everyone can learn, contribute and improve.

---

## Our Standards

We encourage behavior that creates a positive and collaborative environment.

### Positive Behavior

Examples of expected behavior include:

- Being respectful and considerate towards others.
- Using friendly and professional language.
- Welcoming beginners and experienced developers.
- Providing constructive feedback.
- Accepting feedback positively.
- Helping others understand programming concepts.
- Sharing knowledge and useful learning resources.
- Respecting different opinions and approaches.
- Giving appropriate credit to original authors.
- Maintaining professional communication in discussions and contributions.
- Focusing on learning, collaboration and improvement.

---

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors are not acceptable:

- Harassment, discrimination or personal attacks.
- Abusive, insulting or threatening language.
- Bullying, intimidation or deliberate humiliation.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Unwanted personal contact or repeated harassment.
- Trolling or deliberately disrupting discussions.
- Sharing offensive or inappropriate content.
- Plagiarism or presenting someone else's work as your own.
- Deliberately submitting harmful or misleading code.
- Any other behavior that creates an unsafe or hostile learning environment.

Disagreement about code, implementations or technical approaches is acceptable. Personal attacks and disrespectful communication are not.

---

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all interactions related to this repository and its community activities, including:

- GitHub Issues.
- Pull Requests.
- Code Reviews.
- Repository Discussions.
- Contributions.
- Project-related communication.
- Any public or private interaction conducted in the context of this project.

All participants are expected to follow these standards regardless of their experience, background or role.

---

## Contribution and Collaboration Guidelines

To maintain a healthy learning environment, contributors should follow these guidelines:

### 1. Respect Everyone

Treat beginners, students and experienced developers equally.

### 2. Give Constructive Feedback

When reviewing code or suggesting improvements, explain the reasoning behind your feedback.

### 3. Respect Different Solutions

Programming problems can have multiple valid solutions. Discuss differences based on logic, correctness, readability and efficiency.

### 4. Give Proper Credit

Respect intellectual property and acknowledge original sources when using code, explanations or learning materials created by others.

### 5. Maintain Professional Communication

Keep discussions focused on the project. Avoid unnecessary arguments, offensive comments and personal criticism.

### 6. Support Learning

Encourage questions, explanations and knowledge sharing.

---

## Responsibilities of Project Maintainers

Project maintainers are responsible for:

- Explaining and promoting these community standards.
- Reviewing reported violations.
- Taking appropriate action when necessary.
- Maintaining a respectful project environment.
- Addressing inappropriate behavior fairly and consistently.
- Protecting the privacy of individuals involved in reports.

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject contributions, comments or other content that violates this Code of Conduct.

---

## Reporting Violations

Instances of unacceptable behavior should be reported to the project maintainers.

When submitting a report, include:

- A clear description of the incident.
- Relevant GitHub Issue, Pull Request or discussion links.
- Supporting screenshots or other evidence, when available.
- Any additional context that may help the maintainers review the situation.

For sensitive matters, use a private communication method available through the repository maintainer's GitHub profile rather than posting personal information publicly.

Reports will be reviewed as fairly and confidentially as reasonably possible.

Please do not publicly expose another person's private information while reporting an incident.

---

## Enforcement Guidelines

Project maintainers may take appropriate action depending on the nature and seriousness of a violation.

Possible actions include:

### 1. Warning

A private or public warning requesting the person to stop the unacceptable behavior.

### 2. Temporary Restriction

Temporary restrictions on participation in project discussions or contributions.

### 3. Removal of Content

Removal or modification of inappropriate comments, contributions or other content.

### 4. Temporary Suspension

Temporary suspension of participation in project activities.

### 5. Permanent Removal

Permanent removal from project participation in cases of serious or repeated violations.

The action taken will depend on the severity, context and frequency of the behavior.

---

## Fairness and Confidentiality

All reports will be considered carefully.

Project maintainers will aim to:

- Listen to the individuals involved.
- Evaluate available information.
- Avoid unnecessary disclosure of private details.
- Take proportionate action.
- Apply the standards consistently.

Retaliation against anyone who reports a concern in good faith is not acceptable.

---

## Community Participation

Everyone participating in this project shares responsibility for maintaining a respectful learning environment.

Whether you are contributing code, suggesting improvements, reporting issues or learning from the documentation, your participation should reflect professionalism and mutual respect.

---

## Acknowledgment

By participating in this repository, you agree to follow this Code of Conduct and help maintain a respectful, inclusive and collaborative learning environment.

Thank you for helping make **Python DSA Placement Preparation** a better place to learn, practice, contribute and grow.

**Learn Together. Help Each Other. Grow Together.**
